Gym and coach plans are bought on this website, through Stripe. They are bought by the gym for its members, and every fighter and coach in that gym is unlocked by the one subscription — nobody in the gym pays separately.

When a gym plan ends, the gym returns to the free tier: nobody is removed and no training data is deleted, but the gym is limited to three fighter places and cannot add anyone beyond that until it subscribes again.

Prices are in pounds sterling and exclude VAT where applicable. If a gym needs more than 50 fighters, email us.

If a payment fails we do not end your plan straight away. Your card issuer is retried over several days, and access continues while that is happening. Only when the subscription is actually cancelled does access end.
