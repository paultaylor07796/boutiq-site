What's inside

Everything A Fighter Needs. One App.

The depth of a sports-science lab, in your pocket — and built specifically for boxing.

★ The moat

Live Heart-Rate Zones

Real-time zones from a chest strap or Garmin, with live voice coaching in your ears — pace, heart rate and zone, no screen-watching.

⏱️
★ The moat

Interval Roadwork

Build a run like a fight — 8×3 with a minute's rest — and get coached through every round by voice. No general running app does this.

⚖️
★ Boxing-first

Make-Weight Planner

Know your fight date and weight, and see exactly how much to lose and how fast to do it safely. The corner's weight-cut brain.

🥊

The Self-Writing Journal

Sparring, S&C, roadwork, recovery, nutrition — logged in seconds, remembered forever. A complete career history, automatically.

🍎

World-Class Nutrition

Scan, photo or search food, track every macro and your water, set your own daily targets — tuned to making weight.

📈

Progress & Readiness

A daily readiness score, training load, personal records, and step back through any week to compare and improve.

The Coach's Console

Coaches see their whole squad at a glance, message fighters, book sessions, and keep private notes — even across multiple gyms.

Every Device, One Place

Syncs sleep and runs from Apple Health — so WHOOP, Garmin, Oura and Apple Watch data all lands in Bout IQ automatically.
